Where we can fly is set by the FAA

As a licensed operator we fly only where federal airspace rules allow. Areas near airports — Atlanta Hartsfield‑Jackson, Falcon Field in Peachtree City, Newnan‑Coweta, and the smaller fields around Griffin, Thomaston and Hampton — carry altitude limits or need FAA authorization, which can take time to get; a few spots can't be flown at all. Stadiums, prisons, some government sites and areas under temporary flight restrictions are also off‑limits.

What this means for you: send us the exact address or GPS coordinates of the area you want covered and we'll check the airspace before quoting. If a location can't be flown, we'll tell you up front — no charge, no surprises.
